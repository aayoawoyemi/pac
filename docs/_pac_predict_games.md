## Part 3. Game-level prediction when players are missing (cross-fitted within team-season)

34,354 games, 20 random 50/50 splits. Ratings: team-season offense + opponent-season defense + home, fit on one half, predicting the other. Absent players (rotation, inside tenure windows) lower their team's predicted points by their per-game value. 'Big absence gap' = games where one side is missing at least 4 validated PAC/g more than the other (2,278 games).

Two ways to use each number: at face value (the team loses exactly that many points per game), and with a free scale k fitted on the training half (k = 1 means the number is the true per-game cost).
